architectural elements are research approximations. Geographic origin:
longitude -0.1167, latitude 51.5146. Web axes: X east, Y up, Z south, metres.

Geometry and renderings were authored for this project in Blender. They are not
photographs or a measured as-built survey. Do not rely on this project for access,
routing, opening hours or present-day conditions. The Old Curiosity Shop model
reflects restoration photographs published in 2023, not a live survey.

The campus overview simplifies small bevels and procedural shading. Public-interior views
cover modeled study areas only; the MAR web interior omits upper-office slabs that
obscure the ground hall. The SAW interior isolates the spiral stair study, and the
LRB view includes the existing roof lightwell and triangular grid. The compressed
model catalogue records the source hash.

Edition 04 adds Columbia House street elevations and the Connaught House entrance.
References: archived LSE Estate photographs and the 2025/26 LSE Property Handbook.
Upper-bay repetition, roof shape and component dimensions remain estimates.
The LSE logo is reproduced from the owner's existing personal-site SVG asset.
The square favicon uses the original red LSE mark from that same vector artwork.
The mark belongs to LSE; its display does not imply endorsement or affiliation.

Individual detail downloads retain the native evaluated bevels and curve resolution.
Twenty-nine building models and five public-interior studies load on demand.
Building models include the existing authored floor plates, stairs and roof slabs;
this does not establish complete or surveyed interiors. Metric UV layers are
normalized during export to preserve the source brick and slate courses.
Procedural brick/noise parameters are carried as material metadata and approximated
by browser shaders. This is not a texture bake or a claim of Cycles render parity.
Photographic projection materials never export their image or shader descriptor.

Edition 05 adds thirteen first-pass street-facade studies: COW, KGS, LAK, LCH,
50L, 51L, PAR, PEA, PEL, POR, SAR, SHF and STC. Their original gallery renders
show authored geometry, not archival photographs. Street character follows archived
LSE Estate images; heights, bay spacing, unseen backs and roof depth are estimated.
Party-wall identification uses the local OSM footprint snapshot. These studies
remain distinct from the fourteen previously developed detail models.
