The model is a Produced Work using those footprints. Heights, subdivisions and
architectural elements are research approximations. Geographic origin:
longitude -0.1167, latitude 51.5146. Web axes: X east, Y up, Z south, metres.

Geometry and renderings were authored for this project in Blender. They are not
photographs or a measured as-built survey. Do not rely on this project for access,
routing, opening hours or present-day conditions. The Old Curiosity Shop model
reflects restoration photographs published in 2023, not a live survey.

Public models simplify small bevels and procedural shading. Public-interior views
cover modeled study areas only; the MAR web interior omits upper-office slabs that
obscure the ground hall. The SAW interior isolates the spiral stair study, and the
LRB view includes the existing roof lightwell and triangular grid. The compressed
model catalogue records the source hash.

Edition 04 adds Columbia House street elevations and the Connaught House entrance.
References: archived LSE Estate photographs and the 2025/26 LSE Property Handbook.
